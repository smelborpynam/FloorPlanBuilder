Cheat sheet

  • Drag a wall → resize rooms
  • Drag outside wall → resize house, only that edge's rooms
  • Shift+drag outside wall → scale whole plan
  • Drag room onto room → swap them
  • Type exact sizes in Properties
  • Space+drag or middle-drag → pan
  • Scroll → zoom · F → fit
  • Add Wall, then drag → pick its direction
  • Alt while dragging → ignore snap
  • Del → delete selected room, door or window
